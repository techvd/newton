Add `particle_self_contact_speculative` to `SolverVBD`. With self-contact, the penetration-free solve limits every particle's move per contact detection to a fixed fraction of the query radius, which caps every particle's speed at coarse steps, also far from anything (about 1.5 m/s with a 2 mm margin and 4 mm gap at 600 Hz). With the option, each particle's limit follows its own free move, and contacts between different connected bodies are detected far enough ahead to keep that safe. The default stays off.
