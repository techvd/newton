Add `particle_self_contact_comoving_truncation` to `SolverVBD`. The penetration-free truncation fixes each self-contact pair's division plane where the pair started, so two sides moving together are cut short once a step's move reaches the plane, although their gap is not closing; a thin falling body then stretches and runs away (with speculative self-contact, a 1 cm slab at about 4.6 m/s at 600 Hz). With the option, the truncation runs on each particle's move relative to its cluster's mean move (connected bodies joined through their detected pairs), which keeps the penetration-free guarantee. The default stays off.
