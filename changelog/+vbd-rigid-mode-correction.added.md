Add `particle_rigid_mode_correction` to `SolverVBD`. After the iterations, each connected soft body takes one Newton step on its six rigid degrees of freedom against its inertia and contact forces. A Gauss-Seidel solve converges slowest on exactly these modes, so with a fixed iteration count an asymmetric mesh landing straight down gained sideways momentum or spin; the correction restores the implicit step's momentum balance for them. The default stays off.
